Meet the Maker

About Rose

Rose builds tiny worlds by hand in her Connecticut workshop — restoring vintage dollhouses, 3D printing furniture, and hand-painting every piece down to the smallest detail. What started as a personal hobby has grown into a shop trusted by collectors who know a labor of love when they see one.

What I Create

Miniatures for Every Collector

From 3D-printed furniture to fully restored vintage dollhouses, each piece is built with obsessive attention to detail.

Restoration

Dollhouse Restoration

Rose breathes new life into vintage and antique dollhouses — repairing, refinishing, and re-furnishing them to their former glory.

3D Printed

3D Printed Miniatures

Using her home 3D printer, Rose creates incredibly detailed furniture and accessories — then hand-paints every one.

Hand-Painted

Hand-Painted Pieces
